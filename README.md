📊 Financial Intelligence Platform

Transforming raw financial statements into actionable business intelligence through advanced Power BI analytics, scoring systems, growth analysis, and risk visualization.

🚀 Project Overview

The Financial Intelligence Platform is an advanced end-to-end financial analytics ecosystem built using Power BI, designed to analyze, compare, and evaluate public companies through:

📈 Growth Analytics
💰 Profitability Tracking
🧠 AI-based Health Scorecards
🏦 Debt & Leverage Intelligence
🎯 Earnings Quality Evaluation
💎 Dividend Sustainability Analysis
📊 Sector Benchmarking

This platform converts complex financial statements into intuitive dashboards that help users identify:

✅ Strong companies
✅ Risky businesses
✅ Hidden growth opportunities
✅ Financial deterioration signals
✅ Sector leaders and laggards

🌟 Key Highlights

✨ Multi-dashboard enterprise-grade Power BI solution
